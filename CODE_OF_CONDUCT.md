# Code of Conduct

Zero to AI Engineer is for beginners, so this project should be a place where anyone can ask a basic question without feeling stupid.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or AI.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone meant harm.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's background, identity or ability.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening an issue, or, if it's private, by contacting the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Xaib026). Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
